CONNECTING

Dionysus is an AI trader running on the team's Mac mini. He suggests trades; Midas, the bot, decides. Midas only buys a suggestion its own fixed rules approve, and it controls every limit on risk. This page shows what Dionysus said, what Midas did about it, and how he is doing.

HOW A SUGGESTION BECOMES A TRADE
1 · EACH EVENING
Dionysus studies the day and suggests up to 3 trades, each rated 1 to 5.
2 · 9:45 AM ET
Midas checks each rated 3 or more against its rules. Approved ones go first.
3 · IF CASH IS SHORT
For a 4 or 5, Midas may sell part of a winning bet Dionysus named, just enough to pay for it.
4 · EACH NIGHT
Dionysus reads how it went, writes lessons, and may change one of Midas's settings for the next day.

WHAT THE WORDS MEAN
CALL / PUT
A call is a bet a stock rises; a put is a bet it falls. The most a bet can lose is what was paid for it.
CONVICTION
How sure Dionysus is, from 1 (a punt) to 5 (he would bet the desk). Midas ignores 1 and 2.
THREE-WEEK BET
Midas's main kind of trade: an option that expires in about three weeks. Dionysus's picks are always this kind.
SLEEVE
The $50,000 of the class account that Midas manages.
LEAD LOCK
A rule that protects the account's lead over SPY: when the lead is thin, Midas stops opening new bets.
RIGHT AFTER 1 DAY
A pick counts as right if the stock moved its way by the next day's close, whether or not it was traded.
